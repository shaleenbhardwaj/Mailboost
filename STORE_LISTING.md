## Screenshots (1280×800 each)

Shoot all five. The first is the one that sells the install — the store shows it in search results.

### 1. The before/after split — *the hero shot*
A single 1280×800 frame split down the middle by a thin vertical divider. **Left, labelled "Outlook's dark mode":** a real marketing email rendered badly — glaring white content block, a stripe of black-on-charcoal text, an invisible link. **Right, labelled "With MailBoost":** the identical email rendered correctly in true dark with legible text and blue links. Overlay caption across the top: **"Microsoft's dark mode is broken. This fixes it."**
*This must be screenshot #1. It communicates the entire value proposition without any reading.*

### 2. The toolbar in context
A full Outlook window, dark theme, an email open. The dark navy MailBoost toolbar sits at the top of the reading pane with all three buttons visible: `🚫 Unsubscribe`, `🔕 Smart Snooze PRO`, `✨ AI Summary PRO`. A soft callout arrow points at Unsubscribe with the caption **"One click. The tab opens and closes itself."**
*Shows the product is embedded in Outlook, not a separate app.*

### 3. The AI summary sidebar
The 320px sidebar slid in on the right against a long, visibly dense email thread on the left. The sidebar shows a filled-in summary: three bullets, an amber **Deadline: Friday 5pm**, an Action required block, and the three quick-reply chips. Caption: **"40 messages. Three bullets. Two seconds."**
*This is the differentiator versus every other Outlook extension.*

### 4. The Smart Snooze modal
The snooze modal open over a dimmed inbox, with the context note field filled in with a real example (`Follow up about invoice #447`) and the four options visible. A highlight ring around **💬 When they reply** with the caption **"A snooze that waits for the human, not the clock. Outlook can't do this."**
*Names the competitor's gap explicitly — this converts people who already use Outlook's snooze.*

### 5. The settings popup
The 380px popup at natural size against a blurred Outlook background, showing the gold **Pro ✓** badge, the dark-mode toggle on, populated stats (`🚫 Unsubscribed 47`, `✨ Summarised 23`), and the API-key field with the reassurance line visible. Caption: **"Your key stays in your browser. No servers, no tracking."**
*Answers the privacy objection before the user has to go looking for the policy.*

**Production notes:** use real-looking but fabricated email content — never a genuine inbox, and never a real person's name or address. Blur or replace any address bar contents. Captions should be a single readable weight at 28–34px so they survive the store's thumbnail scaling.

## Keywords for discovery

The store has no keyword field — ranking comes from the title, the short description, and term frequency in the full description. These terms are already woven into the copy above; keep them there if you rewrite it.

**Primary (highest intent):**
`outlook dark mode` · `outlook dark mode fix` · `unsubscribe` · `one click unsubscribe` · `outlook web` · `email dark mode`

**Secondary (feature terms):**
`email snooze` · `snooze email` · `ai email summary` · `summarize email` · `send later` · `outlook extension` · `outlook plugin` · `inbox cleaner` · `newsletter unsubscribe` · `bulk unsubscribe`

**Long-tail (low volume, very high conversion):**
`fix outlook white background dark mode` · `outlook reading pane dark mode broken` · `outlook dark mode not working emails` · `unsubscribe from newsletters outlook` · `snooze until they reply` · `outlook 365 web dark mode fix`

**Deliberately not targeted:** `gmail` — MailBoost doesn't support it in v1.0, and attracting Gmail users produces one-star reviews. Add these terms the day the Gmail adapter ships.

**Ranking notes:**
- "outlook dark mode" appears in the title, the short description, and four times in the full description. That is intentional and roughly the maximum before it reads as stuffed.
- Install velocity in the first 72 hours matters more than any on-page term. Line up your launch posts (r/Outlook, r/chrome_extensions, Hacker News "Show HN", Product Hunt) before you submit, not after approval.
- Reviews are the second-strongest signal. The "Buy me a coffee" section in the popup is a deliberate low-friction place to add a "rate us" nudge in v1.1.
